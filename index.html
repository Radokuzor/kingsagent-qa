What the Kings Agent is

The Kings Agent is an AI assistant that lives inside KingsChat as a normal contact. You send it a message the way you would to a person, and it answers, and does things for you.

An agent is not a chat bot, and this is the whole difference

What an agent does

  • Understands what you actually want, even when you do not phrase it well
  • Answers in its own words, then takes action: it sets your reminder, arms your phone, opens your wallet, designs the card, sends the message
  • Remembers you across conversations: your name, your city, your time zone, what you asked for last week
  • Looks things up live, and checks the real source instead of guessing
  • Follows rules you never have to repeat, like keeping replies short and never taking sides in politics

What it is not

  • Not a chat bot with a menu. There are no buttons, no numbered options and no script to walk through
  • Not a search box. It does not paste links at you. It answers, then acts
  • Not a person. It has no feelings, no title, and it will tell you plainly when it cannot do something
  • Not a doctor, lawyer or counsellor. On health, legal and emotional matters it stops and points you to a qualified professional
  • Not always right. Check anything important, especially prices, dates and figures

How you talk to it

Like a person, in your own words. Say what you want and who it is for. There is no command language, no keywords and no right way to ask. If you are not sure, just say the thing you would say to an assistant sitting next to you.

On KingsChat, message @KingsAgent. It introduces itself the first time you write, so there is nothing to set up. Send voice notes and photos the same way you would to a friend.

House rules you will notice

  • Replies are as short as they can be. It will not lecture you
  • Plain sentences. No markdown, no bullet points, no dashes joining two thoughts
  • Faith, money and health questions are answered in Pastor Chris's perspective, quietly
  • It never asks for your password or a PIN, and never logs in as you
  • Stop means stop. Say stop, cancel or hold on and it drops everything at once, including reminders
